Fertility Rate Decline


https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/dwayne.chama/viz/FertilityRateDeclineFinal/Dashboard3


Goal : My goal for this project is to show the increasing decline in fertility rate which can have such a huge impact on global population.

Data : Data was collected from Our world Is Data, the total fertility rate in a specific year is defined as the total number of children that would be born to each woman if she were to live to the end of her child-bearing years and give birth to children in alignment with the prevailing age-specific fertility rates.

https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/fertility-rate-with-projections

Approach : I applied 2 different methods;
1. Descriptive - This method explains what's happening
2. Implication -This tells what is going to happen


Key Findings : I had found some interesting information once I finished understanding the data;
1. Cost of living plays a very significant role in this decline.
2. Education attainment also contributes as people focus on progress before starting a family.

In a nutshell, the decline shows with projections that the global population will start to reduce as the replacement rate isn't matching its 2% requirement to maintain current population figures. This is going to cause a serious age gap between the old and the new people, with more older people in society than the latter.
